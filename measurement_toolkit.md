## 1. Study Administration & Ethical Protocol

### 1.1 Objective & Dyadic Survey Design
This toolkit is designed for data collection from **400 cohabitating heterosexual couples ($N = 800$ individuals)** in urban, semi-urban, and peri-urban Bangladesh. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Dyadic Isolation Protocol:**
> To prevent social desirability bias and cross-partner influence, the Wife's Questionnaire and the Husband's Questionnaire **must be administered concurrently in separate rooms** by two different trained enumerators (one female interviewer for the wife, and one male/female interviewer for the husband).

### 1.2 Couple ID Matching System
Each couple must be assigned a unique 4-digit identifier (`Couple_ID` from `1001` to `1400`). 
* **Wife's Questionnaire ID:** `Couple_ID` + `_W` (e.g., `1001_W`)
* **Husband's Questionnaire ID:** `Couple_ID` + `_H` (e.g., `1001_H`)

### 1.3 Ethical Consent Summary
Prior to questionnaire administration, researchers must obtain written informed consent. Participants must be informed that:
1. Participation is completely voluntary, and they can withdraw at any time.
2. Individual answers will remain strictly confidential and will never be shared with their partner.
3. If scores indicate clinical-level distress (PHQ-9 $\ge 10$ or GAD-7 $\ge 10$), enumerators will hand out a referral sheet to local mental health support hotlines (e.g., Mindspace, Moner Bondhu, or NIMH).

## 5. Scoring & Mapping to SEM Latents

Once the data is collected, compute the scales to match the SEM model variable names in [sem_simulation.py](file:///Users/md.mehedihasan/Documents/Womens/sem_simulation.py):

* **`Depression`**: Sum of the 9 items of the PHQ-9 (Range: 0-27).
* **`Anxiety`**: Sum of the 7 items of the GAD-7 (Range: 0-21).
* **`Gen_SelfEff`**: Sum of the 5 GSE items (Range: 5-20, or scaled to 10-40).
* **`Fin_SelfEff`**: Average score of the 3 FSES items (Range: 1-5).
* **`EL_Frequent`**: Score of Emotional Labor Item 1 (Range: 1-5).
* **`EL_HideFeel`**: Score of Emotional Labor Item 2 (Range: 1-5).
* **`Work_Engagement`**: Average score of the 6 UWES items (Range: 0-4).
* **`Relationship_Quality`**: Sum score of the 4 items of the DAS-4 (Range: 0-20).
