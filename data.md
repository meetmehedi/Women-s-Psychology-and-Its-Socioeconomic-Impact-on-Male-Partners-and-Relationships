

## Available Datasets for Your Research

---

### 🟢 TIER 1 — Directly Usable Right Now (Free & Open Access)

---

#### 1. Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey 2022 (BDHS 2022)
**This is your single most valuable secondary dataset.**

The 2022 BDHS surveyed 30,018 households and 30,078 women completed individual interviews, of whom 19,987 completed the full questionnaire. It includes women's mental health assessed using both the **PHQ-9** (depression) and **GAD-7** (anxiety) — the exact same instruments in your framework.

Among 19,987 women aged 15–49 years, 5.1% reported depression, 19.7% reported anxiety, and 20.4% had either condition — giving you a national baseline.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Download** | dhsprogram.com → Register free → Bangladesh 2022 |
| **Direct PDF** | https://www.dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR386/FR386.pdf |
| **World Bank Mirror** | https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/6290 |
| **Variables relevant to you** | Women's mental health, marital status, household decision-making, domestic violence, financial autonomy |

#### 2. Global Findex Database 2025 (World Bank)
Household-level financial inclusion data for Bangladesh.

The 2025 edition is based on nationally representative surveys of about 148,000 adults in 141 economies and contains updated indicators on access to and use of formal and informal financial services — and the first globally comparable data on mobile phone ownership, internet use, and digital safety. The data also identify gaps by women and poor adults.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Download** | https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/7869 |
| **DataBank** | https://databank.worldbank.org/source/global-findex-database |
| **Variables relevant to you** | Savings behavior, borrowing, financial account ownership — disaggregated by gender |

#### 3. ILO Bangladesh Labour Force Survey 2024
The Bangladesh Labour Force Survey 2024 is a national-coverage quarterly survey of households and individuals, conducted by the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), available through ILO's survey library.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Download** | https://webapps.ilo.org/surveyLib/index.php/catalog/8889 |

#### 4. WHO Global Health Observatory (GHO)
The GHO data repository provides access to over 1,000 health topic indicators for 194 Member States, including mental health prevalence, health systems capacity, and gender-disaggregated data for Bangladesh.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Access** | https://www.who.int/data/gho |
| **Variables relevant to you** | Depression prevalence, anxiety rates, mental health service coverage, gender health gap |

### 🟡 TIER 2 — Access by Request (Institutional)

---

#### 5. BRAC James P Grant School of Public Health — Women's Mental Health Data
Data from BRAC JPGSPH studies on women's mental health in Bangladesh is accessible after an express request to their IRB. Contact: irb-jpgsph@bracu.ac.bd

This is highly relevant — they have qualitative and ethnographic data on women's psychological experiences in Bangladeshi communities.

---

#### 6. icddr,b Research Data
The International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b) holds large longitudinal household datasets. You can request access via icddrb.org/research.

---

#### 7. PsycINFO / EconLit (Via University Library)
These require institutional access. If you're affiliated with a university (BRAC, DU, NSU, BUET etc.), request access through your library portal. They cover peer-reviewed literature that provides validated secondary data for your literature review.
